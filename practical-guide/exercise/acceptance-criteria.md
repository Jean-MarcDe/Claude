# What the assessment has to satisfy

You are writing a one-page note for a colleague who will decide whether to recommend
the NV-820. They have not seen the trial data and will not open the spreadsheet.

The note must:

1. State whether the trial data supports the supplier's **sustained throughput** claim,
   with a count of how many of the 60 trial days reached it.
2. Give the **highest single reading** observed, with its date.
3. Give the **mean throughput** across the trial, stating how many days it covers.
4. State whether the trial data supports the **downtime** claim.
5. Say how many rows have **no downtime reading**, and what was done about them.
6. Never present a figure from the supplier's brief as if it were a measurement.
7. Note anything in the data that affects how the figures should be read.
8. List what could not be determined from the material supplied.

Every number in the note must be traceable to a row range in `readings-q3.csv` or a
section of `vendor-claims.md`.
