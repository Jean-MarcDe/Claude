# Nordvale Systems — NV-820 processing unit

*Extract from the supplier's product brief, supplied with the trial unit.
Fictional. Written for the course exercise.*

---

## Performance

The NV-820 delivers **sustained throughput of 1,200 units per hour** under continuous
operation, with **peak throughput of up to 1,250 units per hour**.

Reliability engineering across the NV-8 series has reduced unplanned stoppages
substantially. In continuous deployment, **unplanned downtime is typically under
15 minutes per day**.

## Operating envelope

Rated load is 1,180 units per hour. The unit will accept higher loads on operator
request; operation above rated load is supported but is not covered by the sustained
throughput figure above, and is excluded from the warranty terms in §7.

## Service

Standard contract covers a four-hour response during business hours. The logger module
is a field-replaceable part; replacement takes approximately 40 minutes, during which
readings are not recorded.
